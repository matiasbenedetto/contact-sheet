# Demo site topic — Concrete Poetry

The Contact Sheet demo site is a photo blog about **brutalist architecture,
geometry, and monochrome photography**. The subject matter plays to the theme's
strengths: a stripped-down, film-inspired design (black palette, photo-strip,
minimal chrome) pairs naturally with high-contrast monochrome images, hard
shadows, and repeating geometric patterns.

## Sub-topics

Each sub-topic can seed a set of demo posts (and/or a category), giving the
demo site variety while staying inside one coherent aesthetic.

1. **Raw Concrete** — Straight-on brutalist facades shot like typology studies:
   centered, frontal, identical framing, overcast flat light.
2. **Stairwell** — Only staircases, ramps, and parking structures; spiraling
   curves and diagonals in deep-shadow monochrome.
3. **Social Housing** — Estates and tower blocks with balconies as repeating
   grids, plus small human traces — laundry, satellite dishes, a single lit
   window.
4. **Cast in Béton** — Close-up textures of board-formed concrete, tie holes,
   and water stains; almost abstract, reads like skin.
5. **Concrete & Sky** — Looking straight up at towers converging against blank
   white sky; vertigo compositions, high-key contrast.
6. **After Hours Modernism** — The same brutalist buildings but at night:
   floodlit concrete, long exposures, glowing lobbies in the dark mass.
7. **Soviet Bus Stops** — Roadside modernist relics in empty landscapes;
   deadpan color instead of monochrome, faded pastels against grey.

## Why this topic

- Monochrome, high-contrast imagery matches the theme's black-and-white
  palette and doesn't fight the design.
- Repeating geometric subjects (grids, balconies, stairs) look strong in the
  photo-strip block and in index/archive grids.
- The sub-topics span textures (close-up), structures (mid-range), and
  skies/negative space (wide), demonstrating the theme across image styles.
- One color-forward sub-topic (Soviet Bus Stops) shows the theme also handles
  color photography without breaking the overall mood.
