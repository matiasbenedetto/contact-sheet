## The heavy image showcase post

**Twelve Facades, One Sky** (post 1)

- Format: title + one paragraph + 12 images (within the 5–15 range).
- Paragraph idea: the same overcast sky repeated behind twelve different
  facades — a typology study where the buildings change and the light never
  does.
- Images: twelve brutalist facades shot identically — centered, frontal,
  flat overcast light — so the grid reads as a contact sheet of siblings.
- Purpose: stress-tests how the theme handles an image-dense post (spacing
  between images, photo-strip, archive/index crops, page weight).
