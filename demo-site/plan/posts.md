# Demo site posts — Concrete Poetry

Fifteen posts for the demo site, following the topic and sub-topics in
[topic.md](topic.md). The fifteen cover **all seven** sub-topics.

Each post has five fields:

- **Title** — the post headline.
- **Text paragraph** — one paragraph, evocative of the title and images.
- **Image grade** — the mood/style/processing the photos share (always tied to
  the sub-topic aesthetic).
- **Image set description** — what the photos actually show.
- **Image gallery** — 5–15 ordered photo descriptions forming a visual
  narrative. Each uses the format:
  `subject, colors, textures & point of view | location | camera & film | aspect ratio`.

One post is the **heavy image showcase** (title + one paragraph + 5–15 images);
it is flagged inline.
