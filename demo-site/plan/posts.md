# Demo site posts — Concrete Poetry

Fifteen posts for the demo site, following the topic and sub-topics in
[topic.md](topic.md). The fifteen cover **all seven** sub-topics.

Each post has four fields:

- **Title** — the post headline.
- **Text paragraph** — one paragraph, evocative of the title and images.
- **Image grade** — the mood/style/processing the photos share (always tied to
  the sub-topic aesthetic).
- **Image set description** — what the photos actually show.

One post is the **heavy image showcase** (title + one paragraph + 5–15 images);
it is flagged inline.

---

## Raw Concrete
*Straight-on brutalist facades shot like typology studies: centered, frontal,
identical framing, overcast flat light.*

### 1. Twelve Facades, One Sky ⭐ heavy image showcase (12 images)
- **Text paragraph:** The sky never changed. Twelve times I stood square to a
  building, waited for the traffic to clear, and let the same flat grey light
  fall across a different wall of concrete. Set side by side they stop being
  buildings and become a family — siblings photographed against the one
  backdrop that refuses to have a mood.
- **Image grade:** High-key monochrome, flat overcast light, no shadows, low
  micro-contrast; every frame graded identically so the set reads as a
  typology.
- **Image set description:** Twelve brutalist facades shot dead-center and
  frontal, horizon level, building filling the frame edge to edge, blank white
  sky above each. A contact sheet of concrete siblings.

### 2. The Building That Stares Back
- **Text paragraph:** Perfect symmetry is unsettling. Stand in front of this
  one and it seems to notice you — every window matched, every fin doubled,
  the entrance a mouth held exactly in the middle. I framed it the way it
  wanted to be framed and stepped back.
- **Image grade:** Monochrome, mirror-symmetric composition, flat frontal
  light, deep neutral greys.
- **Image set description:** A single frontal facade in dead-center symmetry,
  filling the frame; one or two supporting frames pulling in tighter on the
  matched window grid.

### 3. Nine to Five in Béton Brut
- **Text paragraph:** A government block on a Tuesday morning: nothing happens
  on the outside, everything is implied within. The concrete is honest about
  what it is — a filing cabinet the size of a city street, catalogued here in
  the plainest light I could find.
- **Image grade:** Cool neutral monochrome, even overcast exposure, restrained
  contrast, typology-flat.
- **Image set description:** Three to four civic/office brutalist facades, each
  centered and frontal, framed identically to read as a series.

## Stairwell
*Only staircases, ramps, and parking structures; spiraling curves and
diagonals in deep-shadow monochrome.*

### 4. Helix in Shadow
- **Text paragraph:** A parking ramp is a staircase that forgot it was for
  cars. Looking up the core, the spiral tightens into itself, each turn darker
  than the last, until the top is just a suggestion of daylight swallowed by
  the coil.
- **Image grade:** Deep-shadow monochrome, heavy contrast, crushed blacks,
  single-source light spiraling out of darkness.
- **Image set description:** A spiral parking ramp shot straight up (or down)
  the central void; the curve of the helix filling the frame, detail
  dissolving into black at the edges.

### 5. Fire Escape Fugue
- **Text paragraph:** Zigzag, landing, zigzag, landing — a stair is a rhythm
  bolted to a wall. I waited for the sun to cut it into hard diagonals and shot
  the repetition until it started to sound like something.
- **Image grade:** High-contrast monochrome, hard raking sidelight, strong
  diagonal shadows across the frame.
- **Image set description:** Exterior fire-escape and stair runs seen
  obliquely, treads and rails forming repeating diagonals; a couple of frames
  tightening on the shadow pattern alone.

## Social Housing
*Estates and tower blocks with balconies as repeating grids, plus small human
traces — laundry, satellite dishes, a single lit window.*

### 6. Laundry Day on the Fourteenth Floor
- **Text paragraph:** The grid wants to be perfect and the tenants won't let
  it. Two hundred identical balconies, and on one of them a line of washing
  flags in the wind — the single human hand that breaks the architect's
  arithmetic.
- **Image grade:** Monochrome with the faintest warmth, even daylight, grid
  geometry sharp, one soft moving element.
- **Image set description:** A balcony-grid facade filling the frame; one
  balcony carries hanging laundry that interrupts the repetition. Supporting
  frames isolate the washing against the concrete.

### 7. Three Hundred Windows, One Light On
- **Text paragraph:** At dusk the tower turns into a spreadsheet of dark cells.
  Then, near the top, one window glows — someone home, someone awake — and the
  whole anonymous slab suddenly has an address.
- **Image grade:** Blue-hour near-monochrome, cool shadows, one warm lit
  window as the only color/light accent.
- **Image set description:** A tower block at dusk, hundreds of dark windows in
  a grid, exactly one illuminated; a tighter frame confirming the single lit
  cell.

## Cast in Béton
*Close-up textures of board-formed concrete, tie holes, and water stains;
almost abstract, reads like skin.*

### 8. The Grain of the Formwork
- **Text paragraph:** Before it was concrete it was wood. The planks that
  shaped the pour left their grain pressed into the surface — a fossil of the
  mould, timber remembered in stone. Up close the wall stops being a wall.
- **Image grade:** Tight monochrome macro, raking light to lift texture, high
  micro-contrast, near-abstract.
- **Image set description:** Extreme close-ups of board-formed concrete: wood
  grain, plank seams, tie-bolt holes, chamfer lines — filling the frame with no
  horizon or scale cue.

### 9. Where the Water Ran
- **Text paragraph:** Rain writes on concrete. Years of it, running the same
  paths down the same face, leave stains that no architect drew — dark rivers
  of weathering that turn a flat panel into a map of its own history.
- **Image grade:** Monochrome, soft flat light, mid-contrast, emphasis on tonal
  streaking; reads like ink on paper.
- **Image set description:** Close and mid frames of rain-stained concrete
  panels; vertical weathering streaks and efflorescence treated as abstract
  mark-making.

## Concrete & Sky
*Looking straight up at towers converging against blank white sky; vertigo
compositions, high-key contrast.*

### 10. Vertigo, Upward
- **Text paragraph:** Lie on your back at the base of a tower and the walls
  fall inward. Parallel lines, told they should meet, obey — rushing up toward
  a point in a sky wiped clean of everything but light.
- **Image grade:** High-key monochrome, blown-white sky, strong converging
  verticals, crisp edge contrast.
- **Image set description:** Upward shots of towers and corners, verticals
  converging toward the top of the frame against a blank bright sky; a mix of
  single-building and where-two-buildings-meet compositions.

### 11. The Sky Is a Triangle
- **Text paragraph:** Between two buildings, the sky gets rationed. Stand in the
  gap, look up, and what's left of the heavens is a hard-edged sliver — a
  geometric offcut the architecture forgot to claim.
- **Image grade:** High-contrast monochrome, bright negative-space sky as a
  clean geometric shape against dark massing.
- **Image set description:** Looking up through gaps between buildings; the sky
  reduced to triangles, wedges and slots framed by converging concrete edges.

## After Hours Modernism
*The same brutalist buildings but at night: floodlit concrete, long exposures,
glowing lobbies in the dark mass.*

### 12. Lobby Light at 2 A.M.
- **Text paragraph:** After midnight the building goes black except for one
  warm rectangle at street level — the lobby, lit for no one, glowing inside
  the dark concrete mass like a coal that won't go out.
- **Image grade:** Long-exposure night, near-monochrome dark massing with a
  single warm-lit interior; smooth blacks, controlled highlights.
- **Image set description:** Brutalist buildings at night, mostly in shadow,
  with a glowing ground-floor lobby or floodlit panel; long-exposure smoothness,
  empty plaza foreground.

### 13. Floodlit and Alone
- **Text paragraph:** The floodlights come on and the plaza empties. Concrete
  that looked heavy by day turns theatrical at night — a stage set with no
  actors, lit hard from below, holding its pose for the long exposure.
- **Image grade:** Night long-exposure monochrome, hard uplighting, dramatic
  cast shadows on concrete, deep empty foreground.
- **Image set description:** A single brutalist facade under floodlight after
  dark, deserted plaza in front; frames emphasizing the theatrical up-lit
  shadows on the concrete.

## Soviet Bus Stops
*Roadside modernist relics in empty landscapes; deadpan color instead of
monochrome, faded pastels against grey.*

### 14. A Pastel Shelter on the Steppe
- **Text paragraph:** Out where the road runs straight to the horizon, someone
  once poured a small monument to waiting. Faded mint and dusty coral against a
  grey sky — a concrete bus stop still standing for a bus that comes twice a
  day, if at all.
- **Image grade:** Deadpan color, muted faded pastels, flat overcast light,
  low saturation against grey — the one color-forward set in the site.
- **Image set description:** A lone modernist bus shelter centered in an empty
  landscape; wide frame with big grey sky, plus a closer frame on its faded
  paintwork and sculptural form.

### 15. Twelve Bus Stops on the Old Highway
- **Text paragraph:** No two are alike. Whoever built these was bored of the
  brief and turned every shelter into a small argument — a wing, a wave, a
  concrete flower — scattered along a highway that has mostly forgotten them.
- **Image grade:** Deadpan color typology, muted pastels, flat even light, each
  shelter centered and framed alike against open grey landscape.
- **Image set description:** A short series of roadside Soviet-era bus stops,
  each shot straight-on and centered in its empty setting, so the eccentric
  forms read as a color companion to the monochrome typologies.
